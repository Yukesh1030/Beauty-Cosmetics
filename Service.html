Questions we hear a lot

Everything you need to know about preparing for your session, clinical safety, and appointment etiquette.

Every treatment is bio-compatible and formulated without synthetic fragrance, harsh acids, or drying alcohols. We begin every first-time appointment with a patch test and barrier assessment to ensure complete safety and comfort.

Standard clinical facials range from 50 to 75 minutes. Multi-service packages or bridal rituals run between 90 and 120 minutes. We recommend arriving 10 minutes prior to enjoy a complementary warm botanical tea.

We suggest allowing your freshly treated skin to breathe and absorb active hydration for at least 6 to 12 hours. If you have an event immediately following, we can apply an ultra-light, clean mineral SPF tint.

We kindly ask for at least 24 hours' notice for cancellations or rescheduling so we may accommodate guests on our waitlist. Late cancellations within 12 hours may be subject to a 50% reservation fee.
